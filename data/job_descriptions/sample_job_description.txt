Senior Data Scientist - Analytics & Machine Learning

Company: NexaTech Analytics Solutions
Location: Remote / Hybrid

About the Role:
We are seeking an experienced and passionate Senior Data Scientist to lead the development of end-to-end predictive modeling and advanced analytics solutions. In this role, you will collaborate with cross-functional product and engineering teams to transform raw business data into actionable machine learning models and data-driven insights.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, build, and deploy production-grade machine learning and deep learning models to solve complex business problems.
- Perform exploratory data analysis, hypothesis testing, and rigorous statistical analysis on large-scale datasets.
- Clean, preprocess, and engineer features using Python, Pandas, NumPy, and Scikit-learn.
- Query and manipulate relational and NoSQL databases using SQL and PostgreSQL.
- Conduct thorough model evaluation, validation, error analysis, and performance tuning.
- Collaborate with software engineers to containerize models using Docker and orchestrate workflows with Kubernetes.
- Deploy and monitor models across cloud infrastructure (AWS or GCP).
- Develop automated data pipelines and integrate version control best practices using Git.
- Communicate technical insights, statistical findings, and model metrics clearly to both technical and executive stakeholders.

Required Skills & Qualifications:
- Bachelor's, Master's, or Ph.D. in Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or a related quantitative field.
- 4+ years of professional experience in data science, data analysis, and machine learning.
- Strong proficiency in Python and statistical programming.
- Hands-on expertise with SQL and relational databases such as PostgreSQL or MySQL.
- Deep understanding of statistics, probability, regression, classification, clustering, and model evaluation metrics.
- Extensive experience with data science libraries: Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, and SciPy.
- Practical experience with deep learning frameworks such as TensorFlow or PyTorch.
- Experience with version control systems (Git) and collaborative development workflows (JIRA).
- Proven track record of deploying applications or models using Docker and cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure).
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills with the ability to convey complex technical concepts to non-technical business partners.

Preferred Skills & Nice-to-Haves:
- Experience with NLP (Natural Language Processing) and text analysis.
- Experience with big data frameworks like Apache Spark or distributed databases like MongoDB or Redis.
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines using Jenkins.
- Experience building interactive dashboards and data visualizations with Plotly, Tableau, or Power BI.
